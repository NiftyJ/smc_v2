SMC Labeller

Replay the chart candle by candle, with the future hidden, and mark what you see: ranges, order blocks, FVGs, liquidity sweeps and your trades. Everything runs in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

Load an M1 CSV (from get_dukascopy.py or an MT5 export) to start.
Replay. You only ever see candles up to the replay point; the shaded area on the right is the future. → or Space = next candle of this chart, Shift+→ = one minute, ← back. Switch the chart (M1 ... D1) any time: the candle still forming is drawn from the minutes so far. Every mark remembers the replay time it was made at, so the labels never use hindsight.
  • Range (1): drag a box over it. Choose pause / staircase / wyckoff and up / down first.
  • Order block (2): click the candle (its high to low becomes the zone) or drag a box for your own zone. Below the price = bullish, above = bearish, unless you pick one.
  • FVG (3): click the middle candle of the three and the gap is measured, or drag a box.
  • Liquidity sweep (4): click the swing high / low that was taken, then the candle that took it.
  • Trade (5): limit = click entry, stop, take-profit; market = click stop, take-profit (entry is the last close). Select a POI first to link the trade to it. Looked, no trade (K) records a pass.
  • Select (Esc): click a mark to add a note, end it (E) or delete it.
Zoom with + / − or the mouse wheel, look back with [ and ]. Your work is saved in this browser per price file; export the CSV regularly. The results of your trades are worked out later from the prices (labels.py), not typed in.
