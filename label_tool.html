    How to use it

    Moving around. Drag the chart to scroll (right-drag works with any tool), mouse wheel to zoom, drag the price scale to stretch it (double-click it to reset). Timeframe buttons or PageUp / PageDown keep you on the same moment; double-click an empty spot to zoom into that candle on the next lower timeframe (D1 → H4 → H1 → M15 → M5 → M1).

    Marking. Press a tool's key, mark once, and you're back on the cursor (Shift + the key, or Keep tool, keeps the tool). Every mark stays on every timeframe.

    • Ranges (1 pause, 2 staircase, 3 wyckoff): drag over the candles; Magnet fits the box to their highs and lows. Direction is worked out (staircase: its slope; pause: the move before it; wyckoff: none) unless you pick one.
    • Order block (O): click the candle. FVG (F): click the middle candle. Or drag a zone.
    • Liquidity (L): click a swing high or low; the line runs to where price takes it.
    • Sweep (S) and BOS / CHoCH (B): click the swing point; the candle that took it (sweep) or closed through it (BOS) is found for you. Drag its end to change it.
    • Reaction (R): click the candle that reacted.
    • Trade (T): click the entry on the candle it fills on (Magnet snaps to the order block's open), then the stop. The take-profit goes at 12R (change it above the chart, or drag it). ½ momentum (H): click the candle that closes the break, then the stop: the limit goes halfway back, take-profit 10R. The result shows on the chart.
    • Delete: double-click a mark, right-click it, or select it and press Delete. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.

    Setups. Press N for a new setup: everything you mark joins it, and its steps tick themselves off by what you mark and where (an order block on H4 is the D1 / H4 POI, on H1 the H1 POI, on M1 the M1 order block, and so on). Click a step to jump to its mark, or to go to its timeframe and tool. Done (or Esc until it closes) ends the setup; marks made outside a setup are kept as free examples, such as range examples.

    Saving. Every change is saved in this browser for each price file. Press Save to file once and every change also goes into that CSV (Chrome / Edge); next time, Reopen last files brings back the prices and that file. python labels.py --data <prices> --labels <csv> checks your labels and plays your trades out.
