SMC Labeller

Mark your setups the way you trade them, from the D1 / H4 POI down to the M1 entry. The whole chart is visible: you are collecting ideal examples. It runs in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

Load an M1 CSV (from get_dukascopy.py or an MT5 export) to start.
Moving around. Drag the chart to scroll, mouse wheel to zoom, drag the price scale to stretch it (double-click it to reset). Change timeframe with the buttons or PageUp / PageDown: the chart stays on the same moment. Double-click a candle to zoom into it on the next lower timeframe (D1 → H4 → H1 → M15 → M5 → M1). Setups. Press + Long setup or + Short setup, then work through the steps: each step button picks its timeframe and tool and goes to where you are. Everything you mark joins the setup with that step. Steps are optional and can hold several marks (two ranges, say).
  • Range (1): drag over the candles; with Magnet on the box fits their highs and lows. Pick pause / staircase / wyckoff first.
  • Order block (2): click the candle (its high to low is the zone, its open is kept for the entry), or drag a zone.
  • FVG (3): click the middle candle of the three and the gap is measured, or drag one.
  • Liquidity (4): click a swing high or low; the line runs until price takes it.
  • Liquidity sweep (5): click the swing point that was taken, then the candle that took it.
  • Structure (6): click the swing point, then the candle that closed through it (BOS, CHoCH or breakout).
  • Reaction (7): click the candle that reacted at the POI.
  • Trade (8): limit = click the entry on the candle it fills on (Magnet snaps to the order block's open), then the stop, then the take-profit. Market = the candle you enter on, stop, take-profit. ½ momentum = the candle that closes the break, stop, take-profit: the limit goes halfway back to the stop. The page shows how it played out.
  • Select (Esc): click a mark to add a note, move it to another step, or delete it; drag its edges or lines to adjust.
Your work is saved in this browser for each price file; export the CSV regularly. python labels.py --data <prices> --labels <your csv> checks it and plays your trades out.
